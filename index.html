Choose the use case

Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a lightweight layer, sportswear or a finishing accessory. Start in the matching category.

Save the item reference, color, size and stated materials. Similar-looking photos may represent different models or variants.

06 / Direct answers

Before you
follow a find.

Practical answers for your first shortlist or your next comparison.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize item references, categories and comparison notes. The name alone does not establish seller reliability, authenticity, availability or compatibility with a buying service.

Is this an official Oopbuy website or a live spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent editorial discovery guide with links to CAFANS categories. It does not provide a live spreadsheet, an Oopbuy account service or an official brand partnership.

How should I compare clothing and sneaker finds?

Compare the exact model or style, selected variant, measurements, material description, current price and seller terms. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you own. For shoes, check the seller’s model-specific size chart.

Do the images confirm that these items are available on CAFANS?

No. The images are editorial style references. Category links open CAFANS browsing pages rather than verified listings for the pictured items. Confirm availability and item details on the destination page.

How do I estimate the full cost before ordering?

Add the item price, domestic delivery where applicable, service and payment fees, international shipping, and any applicable import charges. Check the actual provider’s current quote and destination requirements before paying.

Can quality-control photos prove authenticity?

No. Quality-control photos can help you inspect visible details, color and measurements, but photos alone do not prove authenticity, material composition or long-term durability.
